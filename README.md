## 🌟 Featured Project

### 🧭 Scout — AI Sales Intelligence Assistant

An AI-powered product concept designed to help sales representatives prepare for client meetings by bringing relevant company intelligence, recent developments, and contextual talking points into one place.

**Problem:** Sales professionals often spend considerable time gathering information from company websites, news sources, CRM systems, and other platforms before important meetings.

**Proposed Solution:** Scout aims to streamline meeting preparation by organizing relevant information into concise, actionable meeting briefs.

**Product Work:**

* Identified user pain points through user research and Jobs-to-be-Done interviews.
* Developed user personas, user stories, and product requirements.
* Applied MoSCoW prioritization to define the minimum viable product.
* Designed the proposed AI-assisted meeting preparation workflow.
* Explored a technical architecture incorporating LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), workflow automation, and external data sources.

**Prototype:** The repository includes frontend screens and interactive demo components. Live AI integrations, production data pipelines, and automated calendar/CRM workflows should not be assumed to be operational.

**Proposed Technology Stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, OpenAI models, n8n, and AWS.

🔗 **Repository:** [Scout AI Assistant](https://github.com/AyushKumarVujani/scout-ai-assistant)

## 📈 My Approach to Product Building

I believe good products start with understanding the problem, not choosing the technology.

My approach is to:

1. **Understand** — Identify user needs, pain points, and jobs to be done.
2. **Define** — Translate insights into clear problem statements and requirements.
3. **Prioritize** — Focus on high-impact features using structured prioritization frameworks.
4. **Prototype** — Explore practical solutions using AI tools and automation platforms.
5. **Validate** — Gather feedback, evaluate assumptions, and refine the solution.
6. **Improve** — Iterate based on evidence, usability, and business outcomes.
